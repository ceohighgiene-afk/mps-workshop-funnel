My story

A skill that helped me give my family more.

I began with a humble salon, big dreams, and a passion for helping people feel beautiful. Eyebrow work became more than a service. It helped me invest, travel, work toward financial freedom, and give my family a better life.

The profit from eyebrows helped me build High-giene Beauty Clinic & Salon. With years of hard work, faith, and the support of my team, we grew from our first location to branches in Talamban, Lapu-Lapu, and Dumaguete. We opened in Lapu-Lapu during the pandemic, then welcomed clients to our Dumaguete satellite clinic in 2022.

I have also had the privilege of serving as an international judge at the K-Beauty Olympic in Thailand and mentoring aspiring artists at High-giene Academy. I hope sharing this craft can open a new possibility for you and your family, too.

Maricel Perales

High-giene Lapu-Lapu opening, December 2020
